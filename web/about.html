About Traça

A personal planning aid, shared among friends. Each morning it gathers public avalanche bulletins, weather-station readings and forecasts for nine areas (Rogers Pass, the Rossland Range, Whitewater, Kootenay Pass, the hills above Vallter, the mountains around Innsbruck, Rocky Mountain National Park, the Mont Blanc massif around Chamonix, and the Urseren valley around Andermatt) and shows them next to slope-angle maps. You can also check a GPX route against the terrain and today's bulletin.

What it is not

  • Not a go/no-go decision. Nothing here says a slope or route is safe. Read the official bulletin for the area, and decide on the day with training, rescue gear and your own observations.
  • Not affiliated with any avalanche service, park or weather agency listed below. Their bulletins are summarised here; always read the original.
  • Terrain models are approximate. Slopes come from elevation models of 2 m to 30 m (2 m lidar for the Rossland Range, Whitewater and Kootenay Pass; 5 m lidar around Vallter, Innsbruck, Chamonix, Andermatt and Rocky Mountain National Park; 30 m around Rogers Pass), which smooth short steep rolls and gully walls and can show forest edges as false steep patches. The route check knows nothing about the snowpack, can miss small features, and can flag slopes that are sheltered in reality.
  • Glaciers are outlined, not assessed. The maps show where glacier ice was mapped (often years ago); crevasses, snow bridges and serac fall are not modelled anywhere on this site.
  • Forecast layers are model output (new snow, freezing level, timing and sun), not a forecaster's judgement. The sun check ignores shade cast by ridges, and route times are moving time only.

Your data

A GPX file you check stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Your map settings are remembered in this browser only. The site has no accounts, no tracking and no ads.

Install it on your phone

  • iPhone: open this site in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. Opened from the icon, it runs without Safari's bars.
  • Android: in Chrome, open the menu and tap “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.
