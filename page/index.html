    Next 7 days by elevation

    How this works

    Canada's GEM weather model (Environment and Climate Change Canada) for the pass: the 2.5 km HRDPS for about the first two days, then the coarser 10 km and 15 km runs, so detail drops after day 2. Temperatures and the freezing level come from the model's air temperatures at heights over the pass, so they show inversions. Rain or snow is split by the temperature at each band (all snow at 0 °C or colder, all rain from +2 °C), at about 1 cm of snow per mm of water. Precipitation is the model's for the pass; the alpine often gets more, and wind redistributes it. Times in Pacific time.

              How this works

              NOAA's weather models for Bear Lake and the Divide above it: the 3 km HRRR and RRFS runs for about the first two days, then the 13 km GFS, so detail drops after day 2. Temperatures and the freezing level come from the model's air temperatures at heights over Bear Lake and the Divide above it, so they show inversions. Rain or snow is split by the temperature at each band (all snow at 0 °C or colder, all rain from +2 °C), at about 1 cm of snow per mm of water. Precipitation is the model's for Bear Lake and the Divide above it; the alpine often gets more, and wind redistributes it. Times in Mountain time.
